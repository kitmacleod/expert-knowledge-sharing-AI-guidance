# Conversation Starters

Version: 0.1.0

These starters are deliberately simple. The runtime instructions choose the appropriate elicitation route.

## Default

> Start a short knowledge-sharing session with me.

## Start from a colleague's question

> A colleague has a question I would normally help with. Help me turn my answer and experience into a useful knowledge note. Their question is: [paste question]

## Interdisciplinary context

> Help me share a piece of experience about [topic]. People from different disciplines work on this, so explore any important differences in terminology or assumptions if they matter.

## Specific topic

> I'd like to share something experience has taught me about [topic]. Ask me one question at a time and help me produce a short reviewed knowledge note.

### UX principle

Do not require participants to understand or select named “modes”. These starters merely give convenient entry points. A natural request such as “people always ask me why we do X” should work equally well.
