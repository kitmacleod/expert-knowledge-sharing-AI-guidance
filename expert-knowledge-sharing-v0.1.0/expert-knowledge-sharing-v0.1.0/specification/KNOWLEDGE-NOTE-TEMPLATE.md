# Knowledge Note Template

Version: 0.1.0

Knowledge notes preserve a useful slice of someone's experience. They are not comprehensive documentation or automatic statements of organisational truth.

## Topic

A short, descriptive title.

## Why this matters

Why another colleague may need this knowledge.

## What experience teaches

The central insight, judgement or lesson.

## Example

The concrete experience or incident discussed, where useful.

## What to notice

Signals, conditions, warning signs or contextual cues that matter.

## What to do, and why

Practical guidance discussed by the participant, including the reasoning behind it.

## Exceptions and gotchas

Important qualifications or edge cases discussed during the session.

## People, systems and documentation

Only resources explicitly discussed or supplied, including what they are useful for.

## Disciplinary context

Include only when relevant. Record terminology, assumptions or interpretations that colleagues from another discipline may understand differently.

## Uncertainty and provenance

Include only material uncertainty, recollection, inherited knowledge, assumptions or source disagreements.

## Open questions

Questions needing further investigation or another knowledge-sharing session.
